# HR_Attrition_Dashboard

##1. Project Name

HR Attrition Dashboard

A comprehensive People Analytics solution designed to monitor employee turnover, identify attrition patterns, and support strategic workforce planning through interactive data visualization in Databricks.

2. Objective

The objective of this project is to:

Measure and monitor employee attrition across the organization.
Identify key drivers contributing to employee turnover.
Analyze attrition trends across demographics and departments.
Support HR and business leaders in making data-driven workforce decisions.
Provide actionable insights to improve employee retention strategies.

6. Sample Business Questions

The dashboard was developed to answer questions such as:

What is the organization's overall attrition rate?
Which departments experience the highest attrition?
Is attrition increasing or decreasing over time?
Which age groups are most likely to leave?
What is the average employee tenure before exit?
Are resignations higher than involuntary exits?
Which job levels experience the highest turnover?
Is there a gender disparity in attrition rates?
Which business units require retention interventions?
What workforce trends should HR leadership monitor?
7. Key Insights

Examples of insights generated through the dashboard may include:

Departmental Attrition

Certain departments experience significantly higher attrition compared to others.

Tenure Trends

Employees with shorter tenure may be more likely to exit the organization.

Demographic Patterns

Specific age groups or employee segments may show higher turnover rates.

Workforce Stability

Some business units demonstrate stronger retention and workforce stability.

Exit Trends

Voluntary resignations may contribute a larger proportion of total attrition compared to involuntary separations.

8. Recommendations

Based on findings from the dashboard:

Strengthen Employee Retention Programs

Focus retention efforts on departments with consistently high attrition.

Improve Onboarding Experience

Support employees during their first year of employment to reduce early exits.

Conduct Exit Analysis

Regularly review exit reasons to identify recurring themes.

Enhance Employee Engagement

Implement engagement initiatives for identified at-risk employee groups.

Talent Development

Provide career progression opportunities to improve retention of key talent.

Workforce Planning

Use attrition trends to proactively forecast hiring requirements.

9. Conclusion

The HR Attrition Dashboard provides HR leaders and decision-makers with a centralized platform for monitoring workforce turnover and identifying retention opportunities. Through data-driven insights, the dashboard enables proactive interventions, better workforce planning, and informed strategic decision-making. By continuously tracking attrition trends, the organization can strengthen employee retention and support long-term business performance.
